Section VIII of the 64th Közgazdász-vándorgyűlés, convening economists, policymakers, and financial leaders to discuss Hungary's euro adoption prospects and its implications for convergence.

The panel examines when and how a common currency can act as an anchor for reform — addressing the conditions under which the euro supports catch-up growth versus introducing new constraints. Moderated by Takáts Előd (Bank for International Settlements).
